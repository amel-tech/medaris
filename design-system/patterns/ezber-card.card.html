The card is the only place two scripts meet at full size, so it is the only place the Arabic leading (1.9) is non-negotiable. Arabic sets right-aligned with dir="rtl" on the paragraph itself; the chrome around it stays LTR, which is what keeps the badge and the source reference in their usual corners for a Turkish reader. The verso is separated by a dashed rule rather than a fill, so a flipped card still reads as one object.
