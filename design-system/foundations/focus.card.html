--ring-focus-brand · proposed

The Figma file's focus effect is 0 0 0 3px #E5E5E5 — a grey ring, three pixels, no offset. Against the white page behind it that is a measured 1.26:1 — not a visible focus indicator for anyone who needs one, and short of the 3:1 WCAG 2.2 Focus Appearance asks for. It is recorded verbatim because that is what the file says. --ring-focus-brand sits beside it as the proposed replacement — sky-600, 4.10:1 on white and 3.91:1 on the page. Adopting it is a design decision, not a refactor.
