96:1 · --font-arabic / --font-ui

Arabic runs at 1.9 leading, not 1.5: the script needs the room and looks starved at body leading. Cairo also covers Arabic and is the fallback, but Plex Arabic shares the Latin's proportions, so a bilingual card stays optically level. Always set dir="rtl" on the Arabic element itself, never on a shared ancestor.
