The two brand marks — pass `mark="madrasah"` for the talebe app (Tedris) and `mark="nizam"` for the müderris management app.

```jsx
<Logo mark="nizam" size={44} withWordmark subtitle="Online Madrasah" />
<Logo mark="madrasah" size={30} />
```

Glyph-only is the default and is what goes inside köşk avatar tiles and card chips.
Use `withWordmark` only in the top-left of a sidebar or header.
