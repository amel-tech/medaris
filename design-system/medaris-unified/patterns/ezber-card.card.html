Notlar

The front is the Arabic. Classical text is set in Naskh at 26px; the Qur'an only in its own face, Scheherazade New. Each Arabic block carries lang="ar" and dir="rtl" on itself, so the card's edges keep Turkish order. The back sits under a dashed rule on the page colour, so a turned card still reads as one object. The state is the ezber status: Yeni, Öğreniliyor or Tamamlandı. The Qur'an card has no harakat switch: the mushaf is always vocalised. The ayah mark is bound to its word with a no-break space.
