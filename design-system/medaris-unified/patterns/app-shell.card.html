Yeni dönem başladı. Derslerinizi yeni döneme taşıyabilirsiniz.

Below 768 the shell hides the sidebar and the top bar, and the AppBar takes their place: the menu button, the mark, the page's name and at most two actions.

The menu opens the nav sheet, a native modal <dialog> at inline-start holding the same NavItems inside .mds-nav--light, and ends with the signed-in person (.mds-nav-user, AppBar's footer), the only way to the settings page and “Çıkış yap” on a phone. Esc, the close button, the backdrop and a followed link close it; focus returns to the menu button.

The Nizam nav is the one the Sidebar card draws: the same items, sections and icons in both cards.

This card keeps the canonical inverse sidebar on the desktop; screens ported from #95 draw it light until SPEC-D3-04 is decided.
