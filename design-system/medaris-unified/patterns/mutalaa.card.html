A class pattern, not a component: .mds-folio, .mds-sure-band, .mds-source, .mds-hasiye. The folio is the one frame in the system: a hairline with an inner cetvel. The Qur'an sits under its sûre band in its own face, and every ayah mark is bound to its word with a no-break space. "Metinde harekeler" acts on the matn only, never on the Qur'an: on the phone the matn has lost its harakat and the verses have not. "Meâl" hides the meâl. A source line reads "Author (ö. hicrî/milâdî) · Work, place", with the work in italic. The haşiye sits in the margin at the desk and under the şerh on a phone.
