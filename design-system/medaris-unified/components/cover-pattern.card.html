التجويد

A course is a bound book. The cloth is flat, with a blind-stamped double frame and şemse, and a spine on the inline-start side (the right side on an Arabic page). The stamp is ornament and never carries status. The covers look the same by day and by night; a 1px control edge keeps the mürekkep cloth visible on the night page. One course keeps one cloth everywhere: the one its manager chose, or FNV-1a of its id mod 4. So two courses of one science may differ. Label ratios are from contrast.md.
