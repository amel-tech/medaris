Inputs are the one control on --radius-xs (6) rather than --radius-s (8) — that is the file's value and it is what visually separates a field from a button sitting next to it. Placeholder text uses --text-neutral-disabled. Read-only keeps the value in primary ink on the secondary ground; disabled greys it and leaves the form. Adornments are aria-hidden, so a unit is also in the label. The boundary (1.47:1 on white) is OPEN-3 and the error ring (1.45:1) OPEN-1: both as extracted.
