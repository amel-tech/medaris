Every table has a caption, shown or visually hidden. Headers are 13px medium in the subtle grey: never bold, never a filled band. Rows are split by hairlines, never stripes. Figures line up (lining, tabular) and numbers align to inline-end.

A sortable header is a button; the sorted column carries aria-sort and turns ink. Row actions are always drawn at inline-end. The owned-record action is “Gizle”, never “Sil”.

A course keeps its own state in one column. Its enrolment states are counted badges in another (“3 onay bekliyor”, “1 yasaklı”). Below 768 a stack table draws each row as a card, with its primary action at full width.
