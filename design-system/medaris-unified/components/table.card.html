Every table has a caption, shown or visually hidden. Headers are Medium in --text-neutral-tertiary, never bold and never a filled band; rows carry a hairline, never stripes. A sortable header is a button, and the sorted column carries aria-sort. Numbers align to inline-end.

Row actions are always drawn at inline-end; the owned-record action is “Gizle”, never “Sil”. A compact region tightens the cells to 8/12. Below 768 a scroll table scrolls in its frame; a stack table draws each row as a card, its primary action full width and the others under it.
