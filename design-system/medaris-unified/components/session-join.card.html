The join action is always the primary "Derse katıl", never platform-coloured. A meeting chip is the name beside a decorative dot (the last one is the link editor's detected state); a recording provider is its name alone, an unknown one its host alone. The meeting link stays behind "Bağlantıyı göster", in LTR mono, with no copy button. Where there is no button, a neutral line says why. Both zones print when the course's differs from the viewer's. A not-enrolled viewer sees the state and the time, never the platform or the link.
