Üzerinde ve odakta

Each week is a heading holding one button (aria-expanded, aria-controls); a course of twelve weeks gets no region landmarks. Every week opens, locked or not yet open: titles, lessons and dates are public, and the lock says what the reason above explains. Several weeks may be open at once. Below 480px of the week's own width the meta moves under the title and the lessons under the medallion. The Arabic words in the summary carry no harakat, because the summary is interface text.
