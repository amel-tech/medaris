One weight, Phosphor Regular: its stroke matches the text stem. There are no icon colour roles: a glyph takes the text role of its place (only a set star and a platform dot have domain colours), and every text role holds 4.5:1 on its ground in both themes. A decorative icon is aria-hidden; an icon-only control is named by the control. Add a glyph by copying its Phosphor path into the sprite and running icons.mjs, never by drawing one.
